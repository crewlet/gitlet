## gitlet policy variants

defer is the main policy: an op waits for an earlier task that a warn or act body_overlap or predicted_conflict note names, or that `who` lists as changing the function. notes waits on the notes but not on `who`. ignore waits on neither (a signature_clash still makes a task wait). half acts on each note it reads with probability 0.5 (the `who` wait is unchanged). actdup abandons duplicates only on act-severity notes instead of judging every info duplicate note rightly. ## Did gitlet's notes name the pairs that really interact?

Pairs of tasks in flight at the same time. Interacting pairs conflict textually, break the build together, or duplicate each other when both are made on the base. Recall: interacting pairs named by a warn or act note; without duplicates, the same for pairs that are not duplicates (duplicate notes are info, so they never count, and most duplicate pairs also conflict). Precision: warn or act pairs that interact. Notes about interacting pairs: the share of all notes (info included) that name an interacting pair. Lead rounds: from the first warn or act note on an interacting pair to the first of the two landing. Duplicates named: true duplicate pairs a duplicate note named, of all true duplicate pairs in flight together.
